A potential science and technology project (code 23/2025/TN) under the priority research area of information technology, automation and intelligent systems, funded by the **National Foundation for Science and Technology Development (NAFOSTED)**. Principal investigator: Dr. Đào Đức Anh.

**Main components:**

- Hydrological – hydraulic digital model integrated with AI and Big Data
- Water level and discharge forecasting tools
- Real-time decision support system (RT-DSS)
- Multi-sector big data platform
- Remote sensing tools on Google Earth Engine for reservoir monitoring
- AI-IoT camera devices for water level monitoring
- Pilot operation in the Lam River basin – North Central region

{:.list-inline}
- **Duration:** 24 months
- **Funding:** NAFOSTED
- **Role:** Lead organization
- **Status:** Ongoing
