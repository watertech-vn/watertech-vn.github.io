A **national-level** science and technology task, code KC.09.06/21-30, under the KC.09/21-30 Program. Commissioned by the Office of National Key Programs (Ministry of Science and Technology). Principal investigator: Dr. Vũ Thị Hoài Thu.

The task builds the scientific basis and proposes solutions for the protection, restoration and sustainable development of blue carbon ecosystems along the South Central coast of Vietnam.

{:.list-inline}
- **Duration:** 02/2025 – 04/2028
- **Commissioned by:** Ministry of Science and Technology
- **Role:** Lead organization
- **Status:** Ongoing
- **Partners:** Institute of Energy and Environmental Science and Technology, Institute of Oceanography, Silviculture Research Institute
