An **automatic water level and discharge monitoring system** for Song Trau Reservoir, Ninh Thuan province. Data from the monitoring stations is continuously collected, transmitted to the server and displayed online, allowing the managing agency to follow the reservoir's water conditions in real time.

**Main features:**

- Real-time monitoring of reservoir water level and discharge
- Storage and retrieval of monitoring time series
- Water level and discharge charts
- Support for reservoir monitoring and operation
- Integrated into the Ninh Thuan GeoPortal WebGIS platform (QLCLNNT)

{:.list-inline}
- **Location:** Song Trau Reservoir, Ninh Thuan province
- **Field:** IoT monitoring, water resources
