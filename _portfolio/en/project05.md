A **provincial-level** science and technology task under the applied research program for urban management and development, managed by the Ho Chi Minh City Department of Science and Technology and implemented under a contract with the Ho Chi Minh City Science and Technology Development Fund. Principal investigator: Dr. Vũ Thị Hoài Thu.

The task was evaluated by the acceptance council on 27/02/2026, rated **Passed**, and its results were officially recognized by the Ho Chi Minh City Department of Science and Technology.
