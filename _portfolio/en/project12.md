iWAT provides professional services to **Nippon Koei** under several water resources survey and data analysis contracts:

- **Support for data collection and analysis for sustainable groundwater management in the Southeast region** (09–10/2022): literature review, collection of abstraction and monitoring data, analysis of groundwater management issues.
- **Data Collection Survey of Groundwater in Vietnam** (10–12/2022): collecting, checking and analyzing groundwater data for water resources management studies.
- **Water use data collection survey in Dong Nai and Bac Ninh provinces** (04–09/2025): surveying, standardizing and compiling water abstraction and use data.

- **Duration:** 2022 – 2025
- **Client:** Nippon Koei
- **Role:** Consultant, subcontractor
- **Status:** Completed
