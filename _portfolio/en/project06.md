A bilateral research cooperation task (protocol) between **Vietnam and the Federal Republic of Germany** on the management and warning of extreme natural disaster risks in urban areas. Vietnamese lead organization: Thuyloi University – Binh Duong Campus (principal investigator: Assoc. Prof. Dr. Đoàn Thu Hà). Main German partner: Institute of Hydraulic Engineering and Technical Hydromechanics (IWD), Technische Universität Dresden.

iWAT is one of the main Vietnamese partner organizations, focusing on:

- Urban databases and high-resolution DEMs
- Hydrological – hydraulic and land subsidence models, AI tools
- Urban data platform (UDP), urban digital twin (UDT), WebGIS
- Early warning and decision support systems
- Pilot in the Thao Dien area, An Khanh Ward, Ho Chi Minh City

{:.list-inline}
- **Duration:** 36 months (planned)
- **Partner:** TU Dresden (Germany)
- **Role:** Main partner organization
- **Status:** Under review
