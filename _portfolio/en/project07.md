An international cooperation project on the sustainable governance of the Cambodia – Vietnam transboundary aquifer in the Mekong Delta under climate change, using GEDSI tools and nature-based solutions (NbS). The project is coordinated by the **Asian Institute of Technology (AIT)**.

iWAT is a consortium partner and co-leads a component (focal point: Dr. Trần Đăng An), contributing to:

- Groundwater modelling and mapping
- Social surveys
- Nature-based solutions
- Training and technology transfer

{:.list-inline}
- **Duration:** 07/2024 – 12/2026
- **Coordinator:** AIT
- **Role:** Consortium partner
- **Status:** Ongoing
