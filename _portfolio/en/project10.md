IT and GIS consulting services for **Nha Be Water Supply Joint Stock Company**, under two contracts:

- **Upgrading the NHABEGIS system**: upgrading the spatial data platform, water supply network management and data exploitation capabilities for operations.
- **Standardizing the NHABEGIS database for WaterGEMS** (former District 4 area): standardizing GIS data, synchronizing network structure, checking connectivity and preparing input data for the WaterGEMS hydraulic model.

{:.list-inline}
- **Duration:** 2026 – 2027
- **Client:** Nha Be Water Supply
- **Role:** Contractor
- **Status:** Ongoing
