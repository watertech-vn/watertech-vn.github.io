**Ninh Thuan GeoPortal** is a WebGIS platform for water quality management in Ninh Thuan province. It integrates, shares and exploits spatial data on water sources, irrigation works and pollution sources on a single map. The system is built on the open-source GeoNode platform.

**Spatial data layers:**

- Rivers and streams: river network
- Irrigation system: reservoirs, canals, intake sluices
- Pollution sources: industrial parks, solid waste treatment plants
- Periodic water quality monitoring network

**Integrated thematic tools:**

- **WQI Calc**: calculates the Water Quality Index (WQI) and forecasts water quality at monitoring points
- **Sông Trâu Reservoir Monitoring**: water level monitoring of Song Trau Reservoir
- **Sông Dinh 3 DSS**: WebGIS and decision support system for Song Dinh 3 Reservoir

Users can search data, compose maps, manage documents and share data with group-based permissions.
